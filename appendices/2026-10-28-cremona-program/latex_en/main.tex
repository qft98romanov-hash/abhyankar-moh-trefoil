\begin{abstract}
This research continuation studies the explicit rectifications of the Shastri
trefoil and a harmonic figure-eight over $\Q(\sqrt{-3})$. The embedding problem
is approached through the distinction between birational and polynomial
equivalence, relative automorphisms, and the removal of poles. We present
working proofs that the chosen rectifiers are wild, belong to the standard
Cremona subgroup, and are stably tame. A family of transfers is constructed,
and we distinguish the specific questions answered by the constructions,
the inputs taken from the literature, and the questions that remain open.
Symbolic certificates use exact arithmetic and are accompanied by
reproducible code.
\end{abstract}
