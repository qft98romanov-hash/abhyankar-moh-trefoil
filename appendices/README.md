# Research appendices

**Researchers: Ivan Dorofeev and Alexander Frolov.**

The appendices provide the full proofs, constructions, computations and research
questions accompanying the original rectification paper. They retain their
stated **under-verification** status. Internal symbolic checks do not constitute
external peer review or a completed Lean formalization.

## Appendix 1: harmonic knots, LNDs and affine geometry

The expanded 28 October edition contains all sixteen sections, including the
precise consequences for Conjectures 5.1–5.3 of van den Essen–van Rossum,
coordinate kernels and slices, the Makar–Limanov invariant, and the remaining
harmonic-embedding questions.

## Appendix 2: Cremona groups and the research programme

This appendix develops the connections with polynomial automorphism groups,
birational rectifiers, tame and wild automorphisms, stable tameness and the
three-dimensional Cremona group. Its working proofs and all-degree claims are
explicitly presented for further verification.
