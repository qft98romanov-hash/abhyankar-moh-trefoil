\section{Register of resolved and unresolved questions}
Five statuses must be distinguished in this work: an original certified
result of the repository; a proved consequence of that result; a new assertion
of the working appendix accompanied by a proof; a finite computational result;
and an open question. Failure of a bounded search is not regarded as a proof
of nonrectifiability. Several equivalent formulations concerning a single
candidate are not counted as solutions of several general problems.

\paragraph{Historical accuracy.}
The planar Abhyankar--Moh--Suzuki theorem is already a classical
result \cite{AM,Suzuki}. The historical conjecture that nonrectifiable
space lines exist is not refuted by rectifying one or two candidates.
Questions 1 and 2 in Shastri's paper \cite{Shastri}, concerning the existence
of polynomial representations of knots that extend to complex embeddings,
were solved by Shastri himself. Our conclusion concerns the specific
conjecture about his trefoil recorded in \cite[p.~1319]{vdER}.

\paragraph{Results not claimed.}
We have not proved the general Abhyankar--Sathaye conjecture, tameness or
wildness of the constructed automorphisms, minimality of the degrees of the
rectifying maps themselves, a general lower bound of $6n+1$ for slice
degrees, or the exact general factorization complexity of successive
modifications. These questions remain part of the research programme
and are not included among the resolved problems.
