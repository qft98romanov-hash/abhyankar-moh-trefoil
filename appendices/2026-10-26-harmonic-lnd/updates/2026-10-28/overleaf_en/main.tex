\begin{abstract}
We consolidate the results of a research branch starting from explicit
complex rectifications of the Shastri trefoil and a harmonic figure-eight.
Consequences for specific conjectures of van den Essen--van Rossum are
distinguished from the general embedding, cancellation, and linearization
problems, which are not resolved here. We give constructive passages to
locally nilpotent derivations, exact minimum slice degrees, formal kernel
coordinates, and filtration formulas for classes of affine modifications.
Rectifiability of an embedded line is characterized by the existence of a
normal locally finite vector field, and finite certificates for the search
for such fields are provided. For $H(3,7,8)$ and $H(4,5,7)$, complete affine
spaces of candidates of dimensions $24$ and $19$ are obtained; local
finiteness and new complex rectifications have not been established.
The appendix includes reproducible computations and precise limits on
every assertion. No motivic methods, $K$-theory, or homological algebra
are used.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Research branch. All material presented in this appendix remains
under verification.} This branch continues the study of consequences of
the known rectifications and their connections with the Abhyankar--Moh
embedding problem, locally nilpotent derivations, the Makar-Limanov
invariant, and affine geometry. The authors consider it useful to share
these interim results, proofs, and computations before verification is
complete. They hope that this direction will develop into a broader
research programme and invite all interested readers to check, discuss,
and participate in the investigation.
