3. Strengthened relative-transfer criterion
Let h=H(x,p(x,y)+m(x)z,y), where (x,H,W) is a relative automorphism.
Then delta_h=h_z partial_y-h_y partial_z is polynomial and locally nilpotent:
after localization it is -m times the conjugate of the original Hamiltonian
LND, and local nilpotence restricts to the stable polynomial subring.
On an exceptional fibre x=alpha, it becomes -G_alpha'(y) partial_z,
where G_alpha(y)=H(alpha,p(alpha,y),y). In characteristic0, absence of
geometric zeros is equivalent to every G_alpha being nonconstant affine.
This is exactly the repository's centre criterion. It implies a relative
coordinate and a slice by the transfer theorem. Conversely a relative
coordinate implies a slice via its companion, and a slice implies no zeros.
Hence all four properties are equivalent within this ansatz. Over a
nonalgebraically-closed field, fixed-point-free must mean geometrically
fixed-point-free, equivalently unit ideal of derivation coefficients;
mere absence of rational zeros is insufficient. No assertion of absolute
coordinate equivalence or arbitrary-embedding rectifiability follows.

4. Minimum total degree of a Shastri slice is exactly7
The independently implemented stdlib/Fraction verifier review_minimal_slice.py
checks all462=binomial(11,5) source monomials of total degree<=6. Their full
derivation-image support has812 monomials and maximum totaldegree9; no output
degree truncation is used. The supplied31-weight rational functional takes
value0 on every image and value1 on the constant polynomial. It extends
linearly over every characteristic-zero field, so the contradiction applies
to slices with arbitrary complex coefficients. The15-term upper slice has
degree7 and derivative exactly1. Thus minimal totaldegree=7 is proved.

The later elementary proof was also checked in full:
Weights w(B)=1,w(u)=0,w(x)=w(y)=w(z)=-1 make D homogeneous of weight1.
Any slice of totaldegree<=6 has a weight(-1) component which is also a slice.
Write it as s0+B*s1+B^2*s2, where si is homogeneous of Xdegree i+1 and has
udegree<=5-2i. Coefficient comparison gives partial_u s2=0 and then the u4,u3
coefficients of D0s2 force s2=c*x^3. Integration yields
s1=-3*c*(u^3-3*u)*x^2+K2(X). The u5 coefficient -18*c*x in D0s1 forces c=0.
Consequently s1=K2 and s0=L1(X)-gamma(u) dot grad(K2)(X).
For the constant symmetric Hessian M of K2, grad(K2)(X)=M*X, and
D0[gamma dot grad(K2)(X)]=gamma^T*M*gamma'=d(K2(gamma))/du.
There is no extraneous factor2. Thus D0s0=1 gives
L1(gamma)-K2(gamma)=u, with zero integration constant since gamma(0)=0.
The odd part of any degree<=2 polynomial in (f,g,h) lies in the span
of f,h,fg,gh. Their distinct leading degrees3,5,7,9 force all four
coefficients to vanish if their sum has degree1. Contradiction.
This elementary lower-bound argument is correct independently of the dual
certificate. Minimal degree is with respect to the stated original variables,
not an invariant under arbitrary polynomial coordinate changes.

5. Harmonic Shastri-type derivation: minimum slice degree exactly11
For gamma=(C3,C5,C7), an independent stdlib/Fraction verifier checks all
3003=binomial(15,5) monomials of totaldegree<=10. Their image support contains
6017 monomials and reaches totaldegree15. All117 supplied functional weights

characteristic-zero field, allowing arbitrary complex coefficients.

An elementary lower-bound proof was independently derived here and by the
lnd_certificate agent, and the two derivations agree:
Use weight(-1) s=sum_{i=0}^4 B^i si, with homogeneous Xdegree(i+1) and
udegree<=9-2i. Write D0=gamma' dot partial_X, f=C3,g=C5,h=C7.
Coefficient equations are D0s0=1, partial_u(si)+D0(s_{i+1})=0,
and partial_u(s4)=0. The u6 andu4 coefficients of D0s4 imply s4=c*x5.
Then s3=-5*c*f*x4+K4. The u6 andu5 coefficients of D0s3 force
K4_z=0,c=0. Integration gives s2=-f*K4_x-g*K4_y+K3.
Since partial_u(s1) has udegree<=6, the u9 andu7 terms of D0s2 imply
K4_yy=K4_xy=0; hence K4=a*x4. Thus s2=-4*a*f*x3+K3 and
s1=6*a*f^2*x2-gamma dot grad(K3)+K2.
Since partial_u(s0) has udegree<=8, the u13,u11,u9 coefficients imply
K3_zz=K3_yz=0, K3_yy=-2*K3_xz. Therefore
K3=d*x3+e*x2*y+k*(x2*z-2*x*y2).
Integration gives s0=-4*a*f3*x+(1/2)*Hess(K3)(X)[gamma,gamma]
-gamma dot grad(K2)+K1. The symmetry of the constant cubic tensor gives
D0s0=d_u[-a*f4+K3(gamma)-K2(gamma)+K1(gamma)]=1.
All gamma coordinates are odd, so the odd part of this retraction lies in
span{f,g,h,f3,f2*g,f2*h-2*f*g2}. These have distinct leading degrees
3,5,7,9,11,13, with nonzero leading coefficients (the last is -1).
They cannot combine to u. This proves no slice of degree<=10, independently
of the finite dual certificate. The upper slice of degree11 completes it.
