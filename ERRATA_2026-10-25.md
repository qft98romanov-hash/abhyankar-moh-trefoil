# Errata and exact audit, 25 October 2026

## Scope

The previous coordinate-only formulas contained 52 sign errors: 8 for the Shastri trefoil and 44 for the harmonic figure-eight. The compact construction and `verify.py` are unchanged. The original verification did not parse the printed LaTeX expressions, so its successful checks did not establish that those expressions had been transcribed correctly.

The errors are substantive in the displayed maps. For example, the old printed trefoil inverse sends `(1,0,0)` to `(-2,-771,-477)` instead of `(-2,-3,-9)`. The old printed forward map sends the trefoil point `(-2,-3,-9)` to `(1,0,8317474560032)` instead of `(1,0,0)`.

For the harmonic figure-eight, the old printed inverse sends `(1,0,0)` to `(-2,1825-1120*a,2064385-2544640*a)` instead of `(-2,1,1)`, with `a^2-a+1=0`.

## Exact differences: printed minus compact

For the trefoil, set `U=X^3-3*X+Y` and `V=Z+8-14*X^2`. Then the forward difference is `(0,0,D(x))`, where

```text
D(x)=2604*x^24+2584120*x^20+84919080*x^16-1089556*x^10-40318*x^4
```

and the inverse difference is `(0,8*V*U^4,-6*U-10*V*U^3)`.

For the harmonic inverse, put `C=X^3-3*X+Y` and `D=Z+(1-a)*(42*X^3-49*X)`. Its difference is

```text
(0, -88*C^3-10*C^4*D,
 -672*C^5+48*C^6*D+16*C^7*D^2-1152*C^8*D-24*C^9*D^2).
```

The harmonic forward difference is listed in `verification/forward3_grouped_difference.txt`; its variable `t` denotes `x*z-y^2+2`, not the curve parameter.

## Reproduction

`python -u verify.py` checks the unchanged compact construction. `python -u verify_printed_formulas.py` reads and compares all twelve printed components. The trefoil reader uses two independent parsing procedures; the harmonic reader uses a strict recursive-descent parser and an invertible polynomial change of variables for the inverse formulas. Both work over the exact coefficient field given by `a^2-a+1=0`.

Before correction, three of six components differ for each curve. After applying the listed changes, all twelve components agree exactly. This is an audit of the two concrete constructions and their presentation, not a full Lean formalization or a proof of rectifiability for arbitrary embeddings.

The earlier GAP and Macaulay2 checks concern the compact construction. They did not read these TeX files. Their logs are retained with that scope.
